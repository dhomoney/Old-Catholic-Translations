English: `493a033`, `english/suarez-de-gratia/prolegomenon-1-cap-2-part-1-003.md`. Latin: `50e5efd`, corresponding Latin path.

1. **No. 2, note 3 — incorrect reference added by the Translator.** Latin: *quæst. 1 lib. 2 ad Simpl.* Current note: “they stand in Book I of To Simplician, quest. 2”. The source’s book/question reference is correct here: the words stand in Book II, question 1, paragraph 4. See [Augustine’s text](https://www.augustinus.it/latino/questioni_simpliciano/questioni_simpliciano_2_libro.htm). His [Retractations II, 1, 2](https://www.augustinus.it/latino/ritrattazioni/ritrattazioni_2_libro.htm) expressly identifies this as the first question of the second book. Suggested fix: change the note to “Book II of To Simplician, quest. 1, n. 4”. Keep the correction of the Retractations reference to II, 1 and the distinction between *quid velit* and the present *quid possit*; those are sound.

2. **No. 2 — mistranslation of the argument for the soul’s own action.** Latin: *nam si volumus, non alius de nobis vult*. Current English: “for if we will, it is not another that willeth concerning us”. This makes the object of another person’s willing the issue. The point is that our willing is our own act: someone else does not will for us. Suggested fix: “for if we will, it is not another that willeth for us”. This preserves the contrast with the soul being moved of itself and the immediately following statement that its motion is spontaneous.
