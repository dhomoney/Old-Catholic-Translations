## Findings

1. **Minor, addition (title).**
   Latin: *In finem, servo Domini David.* (the print, PDF 232, has no *ipsi*)
   English: *Unto the end, for the servant of God, David himself.*
   Problem: "himself" renders the Vulgate's *ipsi*, which Bellarmine's title leaves out (STYLE l.9 and l.26: the Douay-Rheims wording of the words he keeps). Also, his explanation just below has *servo Domini Davidi* as "David the servant of the Lord", so the unit gives two renderings of *servus Domini* side by side (Ps 17 has "servant of the Lord").
   Fix (optional): "*Unto the end, for the servant of God, David.*" (DR words he keeps); or, if "Lord" is preferred for consistency with the explanation, a note.

2. **Nit, wording (v. 12).**
   Latin: *dejiciat de statu gratiæ in foveam peccati*
   English: cast me down from the state of grace into the hole of sin
   Fix (optional): "into the pit of sin".
