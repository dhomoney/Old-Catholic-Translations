1. **Mistranslation / GLOSSARY breach — *passim* is not “everywhere”.**
   - Latin, v. 9 commentary: “sed passim viri belial dicuntur viri iniqui”.
   - Current English: “but everywhere men of belial are called unjust men”.
   - Problem: “everywhere” turns a frequent usage into an unrestricted claim. The glossary expressly fixes *passim* as “in many places”, not “everywhere”.
   - Suggested fix: “but in many places men of belial are called men of iniquity”.

2. **GLOSSARY breach — *pii* must remain “the godly”.**
   - Latin, v. 3 commentary: “ejusmodi pios homines”; “pios homines”; “pius ille”; “pium aliquem”; “pius homo”; “hominibus piis”; “etiam pii”; “pius peregrinus”.
   - Current English: “pious men of this kind”; “pious men”; “that pious man”; “some pious man”; “a pious man”; “to pious men”; “even the pious”; “the pious pilgrim”.
   - Problem: these use “pious” throughout where the fixed rendering is “godly”. The later *pius affectus / pia mater* entry explicitly preserves *pii* as “the godly”.
   - Suggested fix: use “godly men”, “that godly man”, “some godly man”, “a godly man”, “even the godly”, and “the godly pilgrim” as the grammar requires.

3. **GLOSSARY breach — *viri iniqui*.**
   - Latin, v. 9 commentary: “viri iniqui”; “ita verbum belial est verbum iniquum”.
   - Current English: “unjust men”; “so a word of belial is an unjust word”.
   - Problem: the two occurrences of “unjust men” depart from the fixed *iniqui* = “men of iniquity”. The glossary separately reserves “the unjust” for *injusti*. The Douay-Rheims verse's “an unjust word” remains appropriate for the quoted lemma and its exposition.
   - Suggested fix: replace both occurrences of “unjust men” with “men of iniquity”; retain “an unjust word”. Finding 1 supplies the corrected first occurrence.

4. **STYLE §3 breach — Scripture book names in notes.**
   - Latin: “Coloss. II”; “Matth. XXV”.
   - Current English, notes 2 and 3: plain “Col. ii, 3” and “Matt. xxv, 40”.
   - Problem: Scripture book abbreviations must be italicised in notes as in the main text.
   - Suggested fix: “*Col.* ii, 3” and “*Matt.* xxv, 40”. No change to either note's substance.
