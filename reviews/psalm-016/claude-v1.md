## Findings

1. **Verse 12, commentary (minor, clarity).**
   - Latin: *id est, antea projicientes a suo consortio, nunc circumdederunt me.*
   - English: "that is, before casting me forth from their company, now they have surrounded me."
   - Problem: "before casting me forth" reads in English as "prior to casting me forth", which turns the order round. *Antea projicientes* means they who had earlier cast him out. The next sentences make it clear, but the gloss itself should not mislead.
   - Fix: "that is, they who before cast me forth from their company, now have surrounded me."

2. **Verse 16, commentary (minor, doubtful reading).**
   - Latin: *Ratio hujus veritatis in promptu est.*
   - English: "The reason of this truth is at hand."
   - Problem: the sentence explains why two readings (*porcina*, *filiis*) are possible, so "the reason for this variety" (*varietatis*) fits much better than "this truth". *Veritatis* may be a misprint for *varietatis* (one transposed and one dropped letter). If Clark agrees, STYLE 8 asks for the sense with a note. If not, "truth" can stand, but a short note on the doubtful word would help the reader.
   - Fix: "The reason of this variety is at hand", with a note: "so the sense; the print has *veritatis* ('truth'), probably for *varietatis*." Clark's call; it does not block PASS.

3. **Verse 3, commentary (minor, wording).**
   - Latin: *Nam, ut alias monui,*
   - English: "For, as I have warned elsewhere,"
   - Problem: *monere* here is "to point out, remind", not a warning.
   - Fix: "For, as I have noted elsewhere,".
