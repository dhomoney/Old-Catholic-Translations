## Nits (no change of meaning; take or leave)

1. v. 22: "quod iniquus essem et peccatorum amicus": "that I was a lover of iniquity and a friend of sinners". *iniquus* is "unjust, wicked", not "a lover of iniquity". Suggested: "that I was unjust and a friend of sinners".
2. v. 21: "Sed non est magnifacienda interpunctio hebraici textus": "the pointing of the Hebrew text". He means the verse division (the words "These things hast thou done" go to the next verse), not the vowel points. Suggested: "the punctuation (or: division) of the Hebrew text". This is the same point as Ps 48, nit 2.
3. v. 5: "tanquam immobilis": "as of one immovable" is hard to follow. Suggested: "as of a sentence that cannot be moved".
