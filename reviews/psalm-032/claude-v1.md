## Findings

1. **Nit, margin word order (v. 1).**
   Latin: [Margin: Rectis placet Deus, qui est ipsa rectitudo.]
   English: [Margin: God pleaseth the upright, who is uprightness itself.]
   Problem: "who" now seems to point to "the upright". The body text has it right: "God, who is uprightness itself, pleaseth the upright".
   Fix (optional): [Margin: God, who is uprightness itself, pleaseth the upright.]

2. **Nit, wording (v. 9).**
   Latin: *ita ut sit mandatum atque imperium, cui nihil resistet*
   English: so that it is a commandment and a command, which nothing shall resist
   Problem: "commandment and a command" says the same word twice; *imperium* is sovereign command, rule.
   Fix (optional): "so that it is a commandment and a sovereign order, which nothing shall resist".
