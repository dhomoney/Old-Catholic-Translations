## Findings

### 1. v.5, *id est magnificabitur* (minor; STYLE note practice)

- Latin: sed Septuaginta legerunt נגדל *nigdal,* una littera transposita, id est *magnificabitur.*
- English: "that is, *magnificabitur* (shall be magnified)."
- Problem: *nigdal* is first person plural, the verse's *magnificabimur* ("we shall be magnified"), which the paragraph has just expounded. The print's third person *magnificabitur* (confirmed on the scan) is either a misprint or a slip of Bellarmine's. Without a note the reader meets a third person with no subject.
- Fix: keep the Latin and English as they are; add a note: "So the print. *Nigdal* is 'we shall be magnified', the verse's *magnificabimur*."
