1. **Mistranslation/ambiguity — the whole Church, not the whole inheritance, in the Argument.**
   - Latin: “Ecclesiæ, quæ hæreditatem salutis æternæ quotidie in suis membris consequitur, et in fine mundi tota [page break] consequetur.”
   - Current English: “the Church, which daily obtaineth the inheritance of eternal salvation in her members, and at the end of the world shall obtain it [page break] whole.”
   - Problem: *Tota* is nominative and qualifies the Church, contrasting her individual members now with the whole Church at the world's end. “Obtain it whole” instead naturally qualifies the inheritance and obscures this contrast.
   - Suggested fix: “and at the end of the world the whole Church [page break] shall obtain it.” Preserve the page comment between the corresponding blocks.
