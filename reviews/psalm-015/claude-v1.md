## Findings

1. **Verse 8, commentary (minor, wording).**
   - Latin: *ne commovear de gradu meo, sed persistam, et perseverem usque in finem.*
   - English: "that I be not moved from my step, but may stand fast, and persevere unto the end."
   - Problem: *gradus* here is one's footing or standing-place (a soldier's post), not a pace. "Moved from my step" is unclear in English.
   - Fix: "that I be not moved from my place" (or "from my standing").
