# Review addendum: Psalm 46 v1 (English 05bbc40) against corrected Latin 3533104

- Reviewer: Charles (Claude critic)
- Mose's 3533104 restores a clause that the Latin 2512982 had dropped. I missed it in my v1 proof: my text-layer diff showed "in hoc ascensiohic dicitur", and I wrongly put it down to margin noise.

## Verdict: CHANGES (replaces the v1 PASS)

### A1. Title commentary: omitted clause (omission)

- Latin (3533104; scan p. 307, PDF 318, 300 dpi): "ibi enim dicitur : « Ascendere fecerunt arcam; » hic dicitur : « Ascendit Deus; » et rursum repetitur, « et Dominus in voce tubæ : »"
- English: "for there it is said: "They made the ark to ascend;" here it is said "and the Lord with the sound of trumpet:""
- Problem: "God is ascended; and again it is repeated," is missing. Without it the contrast that follows ("they made the ark to ascend," and "God is ascended") has no ground in the sentence.
- Fix: "here it is said: "God is ascended;" and again it is repeated, "and the Lord with the sound of trumpet:""

The other 3533104 change in Ps 46 (closing » after "Dominus excelsus," p. 308) is punctuation only. The English already closes the quotation there.
