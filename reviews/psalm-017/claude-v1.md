## Findings

1. **Verse 33, commentary (minor, wording).**
   - Latin: *promissa Domini certissima et probatissima, ut aurum igne examinatum*
   - English: "the promises of the Lord are most certain and most approved, as gold tried by fire"
   - Problem: *probatissima* is "most proved, most tested", which the gold image needs. "Approved" now reads as "sanctioned".
   - Fix: "most certain and most proved, as gold tried by fire".

2. **Verse 35, commentary (minor, optional note).**
   - Latin (as printed): *« Deus, inquit, præcinxit me virtute, » id est, simul probitatem vitæ mihi concessit.*
   - English: "'God,' saith he, 'hath girt me with strength,' that is, he granted me at the same time uprightness of life."
   - Problem: the paragraph has just said he received "might and innocence"; *simul* ("at the same time") only makes sense if the gloss on the first half (strength) and the second half of the lemma (*et posuit immaculatam viam meam*) stood before it. Something seems to have dropped in the print. The translation as printed is right; a reader may still stumble.
   - Fix (optional, STYLE 6 doubtful reading): a note such as "So the print. The sense seems to need the second half of the verse, 'and made my way blameless', before 'that is'; some words appear to have dropped out." Clark's call; it does not block PASS.
