1. **Mistranslation — commentary on verse 1, standing in the way.**
   - Latin: “sed est ambulando non recedere in via inchoata.”
   - Current English: “but, in walking, not to turn back from the way once begun.”
   - Problem: “Turn back” restricts departure to reversing direction. Bellarmine defines standing as continuing along the chosen way without departing from it; his explanation includes departure in any direction. This distinction matters to his reconciliation of standing with walking.
   - Suggested fix: “but, in walking, not to depart from the way once begun.”
