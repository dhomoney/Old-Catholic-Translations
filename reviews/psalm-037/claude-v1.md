## Findings

1. **Nit, wording (v. 6 commentary).**
   Latin: *et hoc significat illud, usque in finem,*
   English: *and this that signifieth, even to the end,*
   Problem: the English word order is hard to parse ("this that signifieth").
   Fix: "and this is what that *even to the end* signifieth,".

2. **Nit, wording (v. 7-8 commentary).**
   Latin: *ob litterarum similitudinem, quæ nulla est in textu hebraico.*
   English: *by reason of the likeness of the letters, which is none in the Hebrew text.*
   Fix: "by reason of the likeness of the letters, of which there is none in the Hebrew text."

3. **Nit, wording (v. 5 commentary).**
   Latin: *Siquidem Nathan Davidem corripuit, postquam natus fuerat puer*
   English: *For Nathan corrected David, after the child ... had been born*
   Problem: *corripere* in this Psalm is "chastise" in the verse, and the act here is Nathan's rebuke (II Kings xii); "corrected" is possible but weaker.
   Fix (optional): "For Nathan rebuked David".
