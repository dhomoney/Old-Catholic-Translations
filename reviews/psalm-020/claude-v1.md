## Findings

### 1. v.6, *siginificat* (minor; STYLE note rule)

- Latin: Illud, *cum vultu tuo,* siginificat *coram te,* et *in præsentia tua.* (so the print, PDF 126)
- English: "That phrase, *with thy countenance*, signifieth *before thee*, and *in thy presence*." (no note)
- Problem: STYLE: "A misprint of the printed edition is translated by the sense and gets a note." This is the same kind as *mecula* (Ps 18) and *accurratissime* (Controversiae 002), which have notes.
- Fix: keep the English; add a note: "the print has *siginificat*, a misprint for *significat*." The Latin header could also list it among readings kept as printed (for the worker).
