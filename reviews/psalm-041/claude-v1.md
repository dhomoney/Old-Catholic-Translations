## Findings

1. **Nit, wording (v. 13 commentary).**
   Latin: *Miratur vicissitudines divinæ providentiæ in servis suis gubernandis*
   English: *He wondereth at the changes of divine providence in governing its servants*
   Problem: the servants are God's; "its servants" makes them providence's, which reads oddly.
   Fix: "in governing his servants".
