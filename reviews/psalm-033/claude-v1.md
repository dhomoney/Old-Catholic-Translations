## Findings

1. **Nit, wording (title and argument).**
   Latin: *in priore non immutavit David vultum, sed officium*
   English: in the former David did not change his countenance, but his business
   Problem: *officium* is his office or part (he played the king's messenger); "his business" is unclear.
   Fix (optional): "but his office" or "but only feigned his errand".

2. **Nit, margin (v. 17).**
   Latin: [Margin: In hymno de pluribus martyribus.]
   English: [Margin: In the hymn of many martyrs.]
   Fix (optional): "In the hymn for many martyrs" (the Common of Many Martyrs).
