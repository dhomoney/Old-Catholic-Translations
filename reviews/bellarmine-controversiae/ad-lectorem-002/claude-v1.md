## Findings

### 1. p. 51, *ut alia aliam sereret* (minor)

- Latin: partim cum aliis jungerem, et copularem, ut alia aliam sereret, et alia penderet, et nasceretur ex alia
- English: "partly join and couple them with others, so that one might beget another, and one hang upon and be born of another"
- Problem: *sereret* here is from *sero, serere, serui* "to join, link", which follows *jungerem et copularem*. "beget" takes it from *sero, sevi* "to sow" and then doubles "be born of another".
- Fix: "so that one might be linked with another, and one hang upon and be born of another".
