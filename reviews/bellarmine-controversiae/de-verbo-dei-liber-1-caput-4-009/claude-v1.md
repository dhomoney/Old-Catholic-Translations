## Nits (take or leave)

- ¶1: "Then of those which, although they are truly Prophetic or Apostolic, yet their authority was not always equally certain" leaves "of those which" without a verb. Suggest: "Then of those whose authority, although they are truly Prophetic or Apostolic, was not always equally certain and assured."
- Point 3 (titles in italics): a few work titles are still in roman: the Helmeted Prologue (*Prologus Galeatus*), Rufinus's *Explanation of the Creed*, the *Instruction of the Armenians*. Optional, for consistency with *Synopsis*, *History*, *Of Christian Doctrine*.
