1. **STYLE §3 — wrong alphabetical-Psalm reference needs a note.** At `english/psalm-024.md:15`, Psalm xxxv is listed as alphabetical without correction. PDF p. 150, printed p. 139, title argument, line beginning “cticis sunt XXXIII, XXXV, CX, CXI, CXVIII et CXLIV”. Keep the printed reference and add a note that Psalm xxxvi, not xxxv, is alphabetical.

2. **GLOSSARY breach — *gehenna*.** At `english/psalm-024.md:39`, “bring them to hell” should be “bring them to hell fire”. PDF p. 150, printed p. 139, right column, verse 3 commentary, line beginning “justos perdant, et ad gehennam perducant”. The fixed rendering is “hell fire”.

3. **STYLE §3 — use the Douay-Rheims wording at Psalm cxviii, 86.** At `english/psalm-024.md:55`, “For all thy commandments are truth” should be “For all thy statutes are truth”. PDF p. 151, printed p. 140, left column, verse 5 commentary, line beginning “Nam omnia mandata tua veritas, Psalm. CXVIII”. Note 2 already gives the correct Douay-Rheims wording; put it in the quotation and drop the now unnecessary note.

4. **STYLE §3 — the Psalm cii quotation needs its Vulgate note.** At `english/psalm-024.md:63`, Bellarmine has *Sicut miseretur pater filiorum*. PDF p. 151, printed p. 140, left column, verse 6 commentary, lines beginning “prie significat; unde est illud Psalm. CII” and “miseretur pater filiorum”. Add that the [Vulgate at Psalm cii, 13](https://www.drbo.org/lvb/chapter/21102.htm) has *Quomodo miseretur pater filiorum*; the Douay-Rheims wording serves for both.

5. **STYLE §3 — added words in the First Timothy quotation.** At `english/psalm-024.md:69`, “I obtained the mercy of God” adds “of God” and “the”. PDF p. 151, printed p. 140, right column, verse 7 commentary, lines beginning “juxta illud Apostoli, I Timoth. I” and “consecutus sum, quia ignorans feci”. Use the Douay-Rheims “I obtained mercy, because I did it ignorantly”.

6. **STYLE §8 — printed *perviam* needs a note.** At `english/psalm-024.md:91`, “by the right way” correctly gives the sense but has no note. PDF p. 152, printed p. 141, left column, verse 10 commentary, line beginning “dicio, id est, ducet perviam rectam legis suæ”. Add: “Translator’s note: the print has *perviam* for *per viam*.”

7. **STYLE §3 — the Exodus/Hebrews quotation needs its Vulgate note.** At `english/psalm-024.md:99`, the quotation follows Hebrews, but both Exodus xxiv and Hebrews ix are cited. PDF p. 152, printed p. 141, right column, verse 11 commentary, lines beginning “pulum, dicens: Hic est sanguis Testamenti, quod” and “mandavit ad vos Deus”. Add that the Vulgate at Exodus xxiv, 8 has *Hic est sanguis fœderis quod pepigit Dominus vobiscum*, in the Douay-Rheims “This is the blood of the covenant which the Lord hath made with you”.

8. **STYLE §3 — *Deum* for *Dominum* needs a note.** At `english/psalm-024.md:117`, “the man that feareth God” follows Bellarmine, but the Vulgate and his own verse have “the Lord”. PDF p. 153, printed p. 142, left column, first line “homo qui timet Deum?” Add a note identifying *Deum* for *Dominum* here and below.

9. **STYLE §3 — the second Romans variant is not translated or noted.** At `english/psalm-024.md:151`, “Unhappy man that I am” follows the Vulgate's *Infelix*, not Bellarmine's *Miser*. PDF p. 154, printed p. 143, left column, verse 18 commentary, line beginning “meæ, etc.; et: Miser ego homo, quis me libe-”. Use “Wretched man that I am” and add to note 11 that the [Vulgate at Romans vii, 24](https://www.drbo.org/lvb/chapter/52007.htm) has *Infelix ego homo*, in the Douay-Rheims “Unhappy man that I am”. The existing *Sentio*/*Video* note is correct but insufficient alone.

10. **STYLE §3 — the Ephesians note omits the wording variant.** At `english/psalm-024.md:163` and note 14, the wrong chapter is corrected, but Bellarmine's *spirituales nequitias* is not distinguished from the [Vulgate's *spiritualia nequitiæ*](https://www.drbo.org/lvb/chapter/56006.htm). PDF p. 154, printed p. 143, right column, verse 20 commentary, line beginning “Apostolus, Ephes. IV, vocat”. Add the wording variant to the note; the Douay-Rheims “spirits of wickedness” serves for both.

11. **STYLE §3 — the Wisdom quotation needs its Vulgate note.** At `english/psalm-024.md:163`, Bellarmine has *mors intravit in orbem terrarum*. PDF p. 154, printed p. 143, right column, verse 20 commentary, line beginning “piens cap. II, mors intravit in orbem terrarum”. Add that the [Vulgate at Wisdom ii, 24](https://www.drbo.org/lvb/chapter/25002.htm) has *introivit*; the Douay-Rheims wording serves for both.

12. **Latin source-header correction.** The header wrongly lists *multitudine* as a printer's slip. PDF p. 154, printed p. 143, right column, verse 19 commentary, lines beginning “hominis amantis Deum, ne forte Deus multitudine” and “peccatorum offensus”: *multitudine peccatorum* is the correct ablative. Add the omitted confirmed forms *id i. e.* (PDF 153), *perviam* (PDF 152), and *timens Deus*, *quemquam* and *studioso* (PDF 154) to the header's kept-print list.
