## For the Scribe (not an English finding)

The Latin header at 3cdaa48 now glosses *multitudine* as a slip "where the sense requires multitudinem". That is wrong. In "ne forte Deus multitudine peccatorum offensus ad iram provocetur", *multitudine* is an ablative of cause with the passive participle *offensus*: "God, offended by the multitude of sins". The accusative *multitudinem* would not construe. The print has *multitudine* (checked on the scan at v1). It is correct Latin and should come off the slip list, as reported at v1. Clark's English ("offended by the multitude of sins") is right as it stands.
