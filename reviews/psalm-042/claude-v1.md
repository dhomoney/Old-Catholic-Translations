## Nit (optional)

1. v. 1 commentary. Latin: *a quibus cum non posset refugium habere ad homines*. Current English: "from whom since he could not have refuge to men". "Have refuge to" is not idiomatic English. Suggest: "and since he could not take refuge with men from them" or "from whom, since he could have no recourse to men". Sense is already right; change only if you wish.
