## Findings

### 1. Minor: v.5, *misit ejus Dominus* is a misprint in the print; needs a note

- Latin: *misit ejus Dominus salvatorem unum ex Judicibus*
- English: "the Lord sent them a saviour, one of the Judges"
- Problem: the scan (PDF 133, top of the right-hand column) prints *ejus* ("his"), where the sense needs *eis* ("to them"). The English rightly follows the sense, but STYLE says a misprint of the print "is translated by the sense and gets a note".
- Fix: add a note, e.g. "Translator's note: the print has *ejus* (his); the sense requires *eis* (to them)."

### 2. Minor: v.12, the second quotation is John xviii, 30, not Luke xxiii

- Latin: *« Hunc invenimus subvertentem gentem nostram, » Luc. XXIII. Et : « Si non esset hic malefactor, non tibi tradidissemus eum. »*
- English: "... *Luke* xxiii. And: 'If he were not a malefactor, we would not have delivered him up to thee.'"
- Problem: as printed, "And:" reads as a second quotation from Luke xxiii. The words are at John xviii, 30 (Douay-Rheims: "If he were not a malefactor, we would not have delivered him up to thee"). You gave notes for the other wrong references (notes 6-8).
- Fix: add "Translator's note: these words are at John xviii, 30."
